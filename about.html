Our Story

About Icing for Izaac

From tears to tiers — how love, loss, and a leap of faith built a bakery with heart.

Meet Grayce Holloway

My name is Grayce Holloway. I am the owner of Icing for Izaac. I am married to my high school sweetheart and the love of my life, Eric. We have been together over 15 years, and we have 5 beautiful children.

I have been baking for over 15 years, and I finally took a leap of faith to open this baking business in 2019 after losing one of my twin sons named Izaac during my pregnancy. I decided to be the help I couldn't find and use my passion to break the silence on pregnancy and infant loss while baking as a creative outlet to survive the grief and postpartum depression.

It was the best decision I have ever made!

The Story Behind the Name

On December 20, 2017, I walked into a routine ultrasound for my identical twins, not knowing the day would change my life forever. Baby A — Zaden — had a beautiful heartbeat. But Baby B — Izaac — was smaller. And still. No heartbeat at all.

I shared 25 more weeks with both boys before saying goodbye to Izaac forever. The experience broke me in ways I didn't know I could be broken.

I originally had zero intentions of starting a cake business. I wanted to decorate cakes because when I was doing that, I wasn't crying. Each cake was helping me heal a little more from the crippling postpartum depression.

After the response from my first couple of customers, I decided to follow the calling. Izaac was my motivation. I wanted to make sure he wasn't forgotten and wanted to make the name for him that he didn't get a chance to make for himself.

Two years ago, my husband quit his job and put all of his faith in me so I could carry the family. That support changed everything.

Our Journey

From a home kitchen to a beloved Fort Wayne bakery and cafe.

2017

December 20th

The routine ultrasound that changed everything. We learned that Baby B — Izaac — had no heartbeat.

2019

March — Icing for Izaac Is Born

Launched as a home bakery, working around my children's sleep schedules. What started as therapy became a calling.

2021

Alto Grado on the Landing

Moved into our first commercial space in downtown Fort Wayne at Alto Grado on the Landing.

2023

Our Cafe Opens

On April 1st, Icing for Izaac opened its cafe at 243 Airport North Office Park on Lima Road — our permanent home.

More Than a Bakery

We believe in using our platform to give back and support those who need it most.

Breaking the Silence

We use our platform to break the silence on pregnancy and infant loss, sharing our story so other families know they are not alone.

Moms with Loss Meet-Up

In partnership with Mission: Motherhood and Redwood Inn, we host a peer-led support group for moms who have experienced loss, meeting the 1st and 3rd Monday of each month.

Spreading Kindness

At heart, we are a small bakery with a great big mission. We do our best to spread kindness everywhere we go in the Fort Wayne community.

Visit us at our cafe in Fort Wayne or order your favorite treats online.
