Our Story

Get to Know Us

My name is Grayce Holloway. I am the owner of Icing for Izaac. I am married to my high school sweetheart and the love of my life, Eric. We have been together over 15 years, and we have 5 beautiful children.

I took a leap of faith to open this baking business in 2019 after losing one of my twin sons named Izaac during my pregnancy. I decided to be the help I couldn't find and use my passion to break the silence on pregnancy and infant loss.

It was the best decision I have ever made!
